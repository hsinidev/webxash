**Webxash** delivers a fast, responsive, and cross-platform browser experience built with **HTML** and hardware-accelerated canvas rendering.

- **Cross-Platform Responsive**: Fully adaptable across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- **Local Storage State**: Automatic session persistence and state restoration.

Game Overview


Webxash is a browser-based game that challenges players to navigate through a web-based world by solving puzzles and collecting resources.



How to Play


To play Webxash, simply open the game in your preferred browser. The game will load automatically, and you'll be presented with a virtual web environment where you can explore, collect resources, and solve puzzles.




  

    

Controller:


    

      Use the arrow keys (up, down, left, right) to move your character through the web environment.
      Press 'T' to toggle between different levels or modes.
    

  





In each level, you'll encounter various obstacles and challenges that require you to use problem-solving skills. Your goal is to collect resources (such as coins, power-ups, and items) while avoiding hazards.



Tips and Tricks for Success


Here are some additional tips to help you improve your gameplay experience:



  

Carefully manage your resources to avoid running out of essential items.


  

Pick the right level for your skill and equipment. Some levels may require specific skills or tools.


  

Focus on collecting a diverse range of resources, including power-ups and items that provide additional benefits.


  

Experiment with different strategies to overcome obstacles and challenges.
